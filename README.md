## Hi there! <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aemmadi/aemmadi/master/wave.gif" width="30">

I'm a **Data Scientist & AI Engineer** building production systems with Python, LLMs, and ML. I teach what I build — selected as a **Section Leader at Stanford Code in Place 2026** from nearly 2,000 global applicants.

I founded **[Data With Usman](https://github.com/datawithusman)** — a data science agency helping non-tech B2B businesses replace manual work with automation. I also develop AI agents at **Nobel AI** and **Aptiva AI**, and teach Python at **iCodeGuru**.

Currently completing my **Bachelor of Business & IT** at Virtual University of Pakistan, while competing in international hackathons and contributing to open-source.
